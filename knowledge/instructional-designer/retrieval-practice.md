# Retrieval practice (why each lesson step ends with a check question)

- Recalling information from memory strengthens long-term retention more than re-reading (the testing effect).
- Roediger & Karpicke (2006) showed testing improves long-term retention beyond restudying.
- Low-stakes practice questions work; they need not be graded.

## For HelpMate
Each of the 10 steps ends with one quick, friendly check question with instant feedback.

## Sources
- Roediger & Karpicke (2006), Psychological Science 17(3): https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x
- Brame & Biel (2015), Test-enhanced learning: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4477741/
