# Learning styles: what the evidence says

- Pashler, McDaniel, Rohrer & Bjork (2008): people readily state preferences for how information is presented, but there is virtually no evidence for the meshing hypothesis (matching instruction to a style improves learning).
- Aslaksen & Lorås (2018): studies meeting rigorous criteria found very low, non-significant effects; meshing is widely called a neuromyth.
- 2023 review: no studies reviewed supported meshing; educators still widely believe it.
